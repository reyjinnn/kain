Why can the image and the model differ slightly?

Once the AI illustration is available, SISA reads the visible shape and uses the image texture to build a local 3D reconstruction. Depth and unseen back parts are estimated. The pattern model is available before the image is generated. Sewing measurements follow the checked pattern, not the measurements from the image.

Which fabric are the pieces cut from?

All fabrics use the same scale. Outer lines = cut pieces; dashed inner lines = finished size. Images are reduced; use the PDF for the 1:1 pattern.

Solid line: cut pieceShading: stains/holesDashed line: unused areaCross mark: fabric unavailable

    Learn the terms
    
          
    
            
    Seam allowance
    
            
    The extra fabric between the stitch line and the cut edge.
    
            
    Grainline
    
            
    The thread direction on the fabric that must be kept when cutting.
    
            
    Notch
    
            
    A mark for matching the position of two pieces.
